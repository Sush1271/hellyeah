Quick Facts

What technologies do you specialize in?

I specialize in the full JavaScript/TypeScript ecosystem including React, Next.js, Node.js, and Python. On the infrastructure side, I work with Docker, AWS, and various cloud platforms. I'm comfortable with both relational and NoSQL databases.

Do you work remotely?

Absolutely! I'm a full-time remote developer running Arch Linux on a custom setup. I've collaborated with teams across different time zones and am comfortable with async communication and agile workflows.

What's your approach to problem solving?

I believe in understanding the problem deeply before jumping to solutions. I break down complex issues into manageable components, write clean and maintainable code, and iterate based on feedback and testing results.
